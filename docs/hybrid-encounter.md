# QG6: local quantum binary encounters in a classical test-particle plasma

Status: implemented candidate (campaign case QG6, docs/hierarchy-campaign.md;
gate in docs/quantum-microscopic-validation.md). Kernel quantities are
candidates with quadrature/truncation indicators, Monte Carlo rates carry CLT
intervals labelled statistical, and the model switch carries an explicit
interface-defect entry. No rigorous enclosure is claimed.

## Model

Two nonrelativistic point charges, reduced mass mu, repulsive coupling
g = q1 q2/(4 pi eps0) > 0 (electron-electron), relative speed v,
k = mu v/hbar, eta = g/(hbar v), b90 = g/(mu v^2) = eta/k, lambdabar = 1/k.
Screening is declared per model: a sharp impact-parameter cut at lambda_D
(classical) and the matching partial-wave cut (quantum), or the static
Yukawa potential g exp(-r/lambda_D)/r, kappa = k lambda_D. Every Coulomb
logarithm is the momentum-transfer cross section normalized as
lnL = sigma_tr/(4 pi b90^2), the quantity the Landau/Spitzer rate uses
linearly. Physics comes from `gen/kin6d_symbolic_physics.f90`
(`yukawa_potential`, `screened_partial_wave_potential`, shared centrifugal
term); leaves are generated by `gen/gen_quantum_collision.f90`.

Not included: relativistic/radiative corrections, degeneracy and Pauli
blocking (Theta >> 1 assumed), dynamic screening, three-body encounters,
exchange in the hybrid Monte Carlo (see open issues).

## Kernel (src/quantum_collision_kernel.f90)

Pure Coulomb. The quantum differential cross section of distinguishable
particles equals Rutherford at every angle (`ce_rutherford`, reused from Q1);
identical particles add exchange and the Mott interference
(`ce_mott`; singlet, triplet, unpolarized). In partial waves
sigma_{l+1} - sigma_l = atan(eta/(l+1)) exactly (Q1 verifies
sigma_l = arg Gamma(l+1+i eta)), so the quantum deflection of partial-wave
pair (l, l+1) is the Rutherford deflection at the quantized impact parameter
(l+1)/k, and

  k^2 sigma_tr/(4 pi) = sum_l (l+1) sin^2(delta_l - delta_{l+1})

is local in l. With Langer rings sqrt(l(l+1)) <= k b < sqrt((l+1)(l+2))
(area 2 pi (l+1)/k^2) inside k b <= kappa:

- classical cut: lnL_cl = (1/2) ln(1 + kappa^2/eta^2) -> ln(lambda_D/b90);
- quantum cut: lnL_q = sum_{n<=N} n/(n^2+eta^2) (+ partial last ring)
  = Re[psi(N+1+i eta) - psi(1+i eta)] -> ln(k lambda_D) + gamma (eta -> 0);
- the quantum correction lnL_q - lnL_cl -> ln eta - Re psi(1+i eta)
  (kappa -> oo), the Gould-DeWitt/BPS form, carried entirely by the near
  rings l < O(max(eta, 1)).

Identical particles (`mott_transfer_log`): the tagged (forward-going)
particle, theta in [theta_c, pi/2] of the symmetrized cross section with the
classical cut angle theta_c = 2 atan(eta/kappa); quadrature in
t = ln tan(theta/2) on panels resolving cos(2 eta t). Closed-form limits:
classical identical folding shifts lnL by (1 - 2 ln 2)/2; the unpolarized
Mott term adds -ln2/2 for eta -> 0 (singlet +ln2, triplet -ln2) and averages
out for eta >> 1.

Yukawa (`yukawa_plan`, `yukawa_phases`, `yukawa_transfer_log`): phase
shifts at fixed energy on the shared radial FEM-DVR (`src/radial_dvr.f90`
mesh/kinetic band; dimensionless k = hbar = mu = 1, so the coupling is eta
and lambda_D is kappa). For each l the banded system (H_l - E) a = 0 is solved
(LAPACK dgbtrf/dgbtrs) with a = 0 at a bridge node where the WKB barrier
depth from the turning point exceeds 36 (weight exp(-72) recorded) and a = 1
at the first bridge node beyond r_turn + 28 lambda_D; the last element is
matched by least squares to Riccati-Bessel jhat_l, nhat_l (upward
recurrence, stable for x >= l, which the matching radius guarantees);
delta_l modulo pi. The l range ends where the Born-asymptotic tail of lnL is
below 1e-10 (about 11 kappa partial waves). d delta_l/d eta is the
equation-level tangent (H - E) da = -(dV/dg) a with the same factorization
(dV/dg from the generated JVP leaf); the plan (mesh, inner/outer nodes, l
range) is frozen for derivatives and finite differences.

Classical Yukawa (`classical_yukawa_deflection`, `classical_yukawa_log`):
deflection integral in u = 1/r with u = u0 (1 - s^2) removing the
turning-point singularity, Gauss-Legendre in s; ln b panels for the transfer.

Second moment: `viscosity_from_phases` gives sigma^(2) = int sin^2 dsigma =
(4 pi/k^2) sum (l+1)(l+2)/(2l+3) sin^2(delta_l - delta_{l+2});
`coulomb_ring_viscosity` the same moment of the ring kernel below.

## Hybrid scheme (src/hybrid_encounter.f90)

Test electrons in a fixed Maxwellian electron background; binary encounters
with bare Coulomb inside the impact cut b_max = lambda_D (Spitzer screening
model), instantaneous on the plasma clock. The relative motion of every
encounter is integrated with the shared `nbody` KDK step (reduced mass,
fixed source, encounter-resolved step factor 0.02, no softening) from and to
the sphere R0 = 3 max(b, r_det), with exact Kepler lifting at both ends.

Interface (lift / kernel / project):

- indicator: lifted asymptotic eta < eta_switch (default 1) and lifted
  impact parameter b < b_s, b_s = sqrt(L(L+1))/k, L = ceil(4 max(eta, 1))
  (capped at b_max), evaluated when the integrator first enters the approach
  sphere r_det = 2 (b90 + sqrt(b90^2 + b_s^2)), which every b < b_s
  trajectory enters (distance alone is not the criterion);
- lifting: energy, angular momentum h and Runge-Lenz vector of the current
  state give the exact incoming asymptote (v_inf, u_in, b);
- kernel: ring l of k b, outgoing asymptote rotated by
  chi_l = 2 (sigma_{l+1} - sigma_l) = 2 atan(eta/(l+1)) in the orbit plane;
  the ring areas carry exactly the (l+1) weights of the l-local transfer sum,
  so the hybrid reproduces the quantum momentum-transfer cross section ring
  by ring;
- projection: the state on the r_det sphere of the repulsive hyperbola with
  asymptote u_out and impact parameter b' = b90 cot(chi/2) = (l+1)/k; the
  integrator continues outward and the final state is lifted again.

Balances: |g| (energy) and the pair momentum are conserved by construction
(the integrator's energy error, about 1.3e-6, is recorded, not propagated);
angular momentum changes by |b' - b| mu v_inf <= hbar (ring width, recorded).
Both legs of the replaced hyperbola are represented by the lifted/projected
asymptotes, so no near force is counted twice; the far zone b > b_s stays
classical. The naive alternative (rotate v at r_det) double counts the
outer-leg deflection: theta/chi - 1 = 0.112 in the test case.

Interface defects (kernel quadrature, not statistics):

- transfer: hybrid (quantum rings l < L, classical Rutherford beyond) vs
  all-quantum rings: 8e-3 in lnL at v = 1.7 a.u.; rate defect 5e-4 to
  3.5e-3 over the temperature scan (largest near eta ~ 1);
- second moment: the ring kernel puts each ring at one deflection angle; its
  sigma^(2) exceeds the partial-wave value by 10.6% for eta -> 0 and agrees
  for eta >> 1 (-0.5% at eta = 31.6). The ring kernel is therefore exact for
  momentum transfer and an indicator-level model for energy exchange.

Monte Carlo rate estimator (`hybrid_rate_samples`): nu = -<dv_z/dt>/v_b
for a test electron at v_b z. Plain Maxwellian draws of the field velocity
have infinite variance (sigma_tr ~ g^-4); the relative velocity is drawn
from f_M(g)/g^2 (tabulated magnitude, exact direction law, exact weight),
the impact parameter log-uniformly (uniform disk below 1e-3 min(b90,
lambdabar)) with the exact disk weight, and the azimuth is Rao-Blackwellized.
The estimator is bounded; the same seed pairs classical and hybrid samples.
`kernel_slowing_rate` integrates the same models over the Maxwellian
(Chandrasekhar check below); `hybrid_beam_step` advances a beam with
physical (unweighted) encounter sampling for the movie.

## Derivatives

- d lnL_q/d eta of the cut model: closed form (trigamma for long sums,
  generated JVP leaves for ring sums); FD agreement 2e-11 (test).
- d lnL_Y/d eta of the Yukawa partial-wave kernel: implicit equation-level
  tangent per l on the frozen plan; FD agreement 3.6e-9 (kappa = 30) and
  3.0e-10 (kappa = 100, demo).
- d lnL/d eta of the hybrid transfer at fixed kappa and L: generated JVP
  leaves; FD (in g) agreement 3e-10. L is piecewise constant in eta (a
  frozen design choice); d(rate)/dT is not provided.

## Open issues

- Exchange is evaluated at the kernel level (Mott) but the hybrid Monte Carlo
  treats test and field electrons as distinguishable; the tagged-particle
  Mott transfer is not l-local, so it needs a different (angle-local)
  promoted kernel. Pauli blocking is out of scope (Theta >> 1).
- The ring kernel is exact for momentum transfer only; energy-exchange
  (second-moment) defect up to 10.6% at eta << 1. An exact sampled angular
  kernel needs the nonlocal partial-wave amplitude and an aperture-free
  near/far split.
- The Monte Carlo uses the cut (Spitzer) screening model; a screened
  classical force in nbody would allow a Yukawa-consistent hybrid against
  the Yukawa partial-wave kernel.
- Complex digamma/trigamma, Riccati-Bessel recurrences and the classical
  deflection quadrature are generic numerics kept private here; they belong
  in FortNum. FortNum `legendre_q` uses upward recurrence for the minimal
  solution Q_l and loses accuracy for large l near x = 1: at
  x = 1 + 1/1800 (Born phases, kappa = 30) the relative error is 8e-10 at
  l = 200, 6e-7 at l = 300 and 5e-4 at l = 400 (against a quad-precision
  Miller recurrence); the test uses its own Miller recurrence. Next action:
  backward-recurrence fix with a reproducer in FortNum.
- No rigorous enclosure: kernel numbers are candidates with indicators;
  d(rate)/dT is not provided.

# Programme estimates

The estimates supplied during design are planning scenarios, not evidence of
achieved productivity or delivery commitments. The proposed mature application
is approximately 200–350 kSLOC, with a controlled reusable stack of 450–800
kSLOC, another 300–700 kSLOC of tests/examples/oracles, and potentially millions
of generated lines. The supplied subsystem ranges actually sum to 220–410
kSLOC; use that discrepancy as a reason to revisit estimates as scope settles.

Generated code reduces handwritten mathematical duplication, but still has
compilation, binary-size, testing, provenance and audit costs. Track those
costs separately. Verified physics coverage and maintenance burden matter
more than line count.

The conventional-development scenario was 80–150 expert person-years over
10–15 calendar years. The AI-assisted scenario proposed 2–4 scientific leads,
3–6 months for a teaching platform, 1–1.5 years for DK/moment research,
2–3 years for a GK/FK multiphysics platform, and 3–5 years for most of the
long-range envelope. These numbers are unvalidated hypotheses. Do not infer
scientific readiness or a rigorous theorem from source-production speed.

Re-estimate from measured milestone effort, independent validation, certificate
closure, representative runtime and memory, and real-device evidence. Keep
architectural authority with one controller; parallel work, when explicitly
authorized, addresses bounded leaves and returns evidence. Never allow many
workers to invent competing mathematical interfaces.
